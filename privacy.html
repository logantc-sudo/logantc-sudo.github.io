A Little Handier Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy covers A Little Handier for iPhone, this privacy and support website, and optional support correspondence. The initial release is available only in the United States. It is free and ad-supported. A Little Handier is designed for adult DIY users and is not directed or marketed to children.

Local guide data

The app has no accounts. Guides and photos are included with the app and can be used offline. The app does not upload your guide activity to us.

Your favorites and recent history are stored in local preferences on your device: the identifiers of your favorite guides and your 10 most recently opened guides. These records do not include timestamps or a user identifier. Searches are processed in memory and are not saved. The app does not send these records or search terms to a server or advertising provider.

In Privacy and local data, you can clear favorites or recent history separately. Each action asks for confirmation and can be canceled; clearing one collection preserves the other. If a storage change fails, the app reports it and older data may return when you reopen the app. Device backups or restores may retain or restore copies that these controls do not erase.

These local-data controls do not delete support emails, TestFlight records, website-host records, or advertising-provider records. They do not change advertising consent. We cannot remotely clear your device's local favorites or history. The app does not pass favorites, history, search terms, or support email to ad targeting.

Optional support email

If you email alhprocs@gmail.com, we receive your email address, any name you provide, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and investigate the issue you report. Our support inbox uses Gmail, provided by Google; your own email provider also processes the correspondence. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Support email is optional and separate from the app's local storage. The app does not automatically attach your favorites or history. Please do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

TestFlight beta testing

When you use TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. Your name and email address may be included depending on how you were invited. Feedback and screenshots you choose to submit are also shared. We use beta information to investigate issues and improve A Little Handier. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

This is Apple's beta-testing service, separate from the app's local guide data. Apple controls its own processing and retention; deleting a copy we hold does not delete Apple's records.

Retention and deletion

We keep support correspondence and any beta reports we download while investigating the issue. We delete our copies within 90 days after the issue is closed, unless an unresolved dispute or legal requirement requires longer retention.

You can request access to or deletion of information we hold by emailing alhprocs@gmail.com. Google, Apple, and GitHub control their own service records and backups under their respective policies. Deleting our copies does not delete those providers' records or device backups.

This website

This privacy and support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, contact forms, or tracking cookies to these pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other websites are subject to those websites' privacy practices. Email links open your email app and do not send a message automatically. This website's hosting is separate from the native iPhone app's local storage.

Advertising and Google AdMob

The app includes a Google AdMob banner on the Home screen only, adapting to the available width when the screen layout permits.

The app always requests nonpersonalized ads. Google's publisher first-party ID is disabled in native code before Google Mobile Ads is initialized, and Google's maximum ad-content rating is set to G. The app does not request Apple's tracking permission or authorization to use the advertising identifier (IDFA). No additional advertising-network SDK or separate analytics SDK is included. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may perform its own analytics.

Google describes potential processing by its Mobile Ads SDK of:

  • IP addresses and approximate location derived from IP addresses.
  • Device identifiers, including app- or developer-scoped identifiers.
  • Ads shown or impressions, ad interactions, app launches, and other product interactions.
  • Crash logs, performance information, and other diagnostic data.

These data may support advertising, reporting and measurement, SDK analytics, service improvement, and fraud prevention. Nonpersonalized ads can still use identifiers and require consent where applicable. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Nonpersonalized requests and the absence of a tracking prompt do not mean that no data is collected or that no identifiers are used.

Advertising and privacy choices

The consent check normally starts when the Home screen first becomes eligible to display its banner during an app session. It does not necessarily run immediately at launch. The app refreshes consent information through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), presents required messages, and checks whether ads may be requested before initializing Google Mobile Ads and before each banner request.

When required, Privacy and local data → Ad privacy options lets you review or change advertising choices. If the check has not made ads ready, Retry ad privacy check is available when no check is in progress. It can start the consent check again. Messages and choices can vary by region and settings.

The US privacy message offers Allow and Don't sell or share choices. For Allow, the message explains: “This choice allows the sale or sharing of your data. A Little Handier still requests nonpersonalized ads.” The app always requests nonpersonalized ads regardless of your sale or sharing choice.

Google’s consent service may process approximate-location, performance, and product-interaction information to provide consent-related functionality. It is separate from the advertising service, and consent requests can process information even when no banner is displayed.

Advertising choices are separate from Apple's tracking permission and from clearing favorites or recent history. The app is designed to keep bundled guides usable offline and when consent or ad loading fails, without requiring agreement to tracking.
