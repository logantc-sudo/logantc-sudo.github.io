A Little Handier Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone app, including ad-free build 2 and test-ads build 3, this privacy and support website, and optional support correspondence. You can find your app version and build under Privacy and local data.

Build 2 and local guide data

Build 2 has no accounts, ads, analytics service, or app backend. Guides and photos are included with the app and can be used offline. The app does not upload your guide activity to us.

Your favorites and recent history are stored in local preferences on your device: the identifiers of your favorite guides and your 10 most recently opened guides. These records do not include timestamps or a user identifier. Searches are processed in memory and are not saved. The app does not send these records or search terms to a server or advertising provider.

In Privacy and local data, you can clear favorites or recent history separately. Each action asks for confirmation and can be canceled; clearing one collection preserves the other. If a storage change fails, the app reports it and older data may return when you reopen the app. Device backups or restores may retain or restore copies that these controls do not erase.

These local-data controls do not delete support emails, TestFlight records, or website-host records. In build 3, they also do not change advertising consent or delete advertising-provider records. We cannot remotely clear your device's local favorites or history. Build 3 keeps the same local guide storage; the app does not pass favorites, history, search terms, or support email to ad targeting.

Optional support email

If you email alhprocs@gmail.com, we receive your email address, any name you provide, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and investigate the issue you report. Our support inbox uses Gmail, provided by Google; your own email provider also processes the correspondence. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Support email is optional and separate from the app's local storage. The app does not automatically attach your favorites or history. Please do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

TestFlight beta testing

When you use TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. Your name and email address may be included depending on how you were invited. Feedback and screenshots you choose to submit are also shared. We use beta information to investigate issues and improve A Little Handier. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

This is Apple's beta-testing service, separate from the app's local guide data. Apple controls its own processing and retention; deleting a copy we hold does not delete Apple's records.

Retention and deletion

We keep support correspondence and any beta reports we download while investigating the issue. We delete our copies within 90 days after the issue is closed, unless an unresolved dispute or legal requirement requires longer retention.

You can request access to or deletion of information we hold by emailing alhprocs@gmail.com. Google, Apple, and GitHub control their own service records and backups under their respective policies. Deleting our copies does not delete those providers' records or device backups.

This website

This privacy and support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, contact forms, or tracking cookies to these pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other websites are subject to those websites' privacy practices. Email links open your email app and do not send a message automatically. This website's hosting is separate from the native iPhone app's local storage.

TestFlight build 3 with test ads

Build 3 includes a Google AdMob test banner on the Home screen only, adapting to the available width when the screen layout permits. It uses a Google-provided test ad unit, not production ads. Production advertising remains disabled pending review. These changes are not in build 2.

Build 3 uses the Google Mobile Ads Flutter plugin 9.2.0, native Google Mobile Ads SDK 13.11.0, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 3.1.0. It requests nonpersonalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party ID, and sets Google's maximum ad-content rating to G. No separate analytics SDK or additional advertising network is included. Google's advertising SDK can still perform its own analytics. The app does not request Apple's tracking permission. These settings do not eliminate all device identifiers or establish that every SDK data flow is free of tracking.

Test ads use Google's advertising SDK and do not establish that no data is processed. Google describes potential SDK processing of:

  • IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location.
  • Device identifiers, including app- or developer-scoped identifiers.
  • Ads shown, ad interactions, and other product interactions such as app launches.
  • Crash logs, performance information, and other diagnostic data.

These data may support advertising, measurement, SDK analytics, service improvement, and fraud prevention. Nonpersonalized ads can still use identifiers and require consent where applicable. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The Mobile Ads SDK's privacy manifest declares device-identifier data that can be linked to a user and used for tracking. That SDK-level declaration is not, by itself, proof that the current test-ads configuration performs tracking. The build's actual data flows and privacy disclosures remain under review; we do not describe this build as collecting no data or using no identifiers.

Consent and local-data choices in build 3

In build 3, the consent check normally starts when the Home screen first becomes eligible to display its banner during an app session. It does not necessarily run immediately at launch. The app refreshes consent information through UMP, presents required messages, and checks whether ads may be requested before initializing the ad service or requesting a banner.

When required, Privacy and local data → Ad privacy options lets you review or change advertising choices. If the check has not made ads ready, Retry ad privacy check is available when no check is in progress. It can start the consent check again. Messages and choices can vary by region and settings. These controls are part of build 3, not build 2. On-device checks remain pending as part of Owner Testing.

UMP also declares approximate-location, performance, and product-interaction data for consent-related app functionality. Its privacy manifest marks those entries as not linked to identity and not used for tracking. UMP is separate from the Mobile Ads SDK, and consent requests can process data even when no banner is displayed.

Advertising choices are separate from Apple's tracking permission and from clearing favorites or recent history. Nonpersonalized requests or the absence of a tracking prompt do not mean no data is collected. Build 3 is designed to keep bundled guides usable offline and when consent or ad loading fails, without requiring agreement to tracking.

Future public release

The first public release is planned for US-only availability and a general audience, not as a children's app. Before a public ad-supported release, we will reconcile this policy, support instructions, and applicable App Store privacy disclosures with its verified SDKs and behavior. The test-ads configuration is not confirmation that production ads are enabled or that the final app performs no tracking.
