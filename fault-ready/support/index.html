Help with Fault Ready

Practice electrical troubleshooting in an offline simulation. Choose Learn for component basics or Practical to explore an operating circuit and investigate faults.

Contact support

Email alhprocs@gmail.com.

Tell us your app version, device and iOS version, what you tapped, and any message shown. For a reading question, include the view, probe positions, meter function, and switch or isolation state. Screenshots help, but remove personal information and share only material you are authorized to share.

Saved progress

Unfinished cases and completed results are saved on your device when local storage is available. Return to an unfinished case from Faulty Systems. To delete a completed result, expand Attempt details and use its Delete button, then confirm. This does not delete unfinished cases or all app data.

Fault Ready does not provide cloud sync or automatically import progress from the website or another device. Pay attention to save warnings; do not rely on the app as your only record.

Simulation limits

The app does not connect to real equipment. Its readings use simplified electrical models, and simulated isolation does not verify that real equipment is safe. Grades reflect performance in the simulation, not professional competence.
