Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy describes the current free version of Fault Ready, provided by Logan Wesney.

Offline simulator

The simulator runs from files bundled with the app. It does not require an account or an internet connection. This version has no payments, in-app purchases, advertising, or analytics service built into the simulator.

Information stored on your device

The app saves unfinished case progress, including time, circuit and meter settings, readings, and component changes. It also saves completed results, including time, simulated component cost, grading information and completion date, plus a recent-case preference used when choosing a new case.

This information is stored in the app’s local web storage. Fault Ready does not send it to a developer server or offer cloud sync. It does not import progress from the website or another device. Device backup and restore behavior is managed by Apple and your device settings.

Managing your results

In Practical → Faulty Systems → Attempt details, you can delete an individual completed result after confirming. This does not delete unfinished cases or all app data. There is currently no in-app option to erase all progress.

Saving can fail if local storage is unavailable or full. Pay attention to save warnings; do not rely on this app as your only record.

Support messages

If you contact alhprocs@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use that information to respond to your request. The email service handles these messages separately from the offline simulator. We retain support correspondence for 90 days after the issue is closed, unless longer retention is needed to resolve a dispute or meet legal obligations. You can contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence; dispute-related or legally required information may need to be retained. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Apple services

Apple may handle App Store, device, crash or TestFlight information under its own settings and policies. This policy describes Fault Ready’s simulator and support contact; it is not a promise that Apple collects no information.

This website

These support pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, account forms or embedded third-party content. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security when serving these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices. Website delivery is separate from the offline app, which does not automatically send your simulator records to this website.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change. Questions: alhprocs@gmail.com.
