This is the privacy and support website for A Little Handier. The current TestFlight beta (build 2) includes offline guides and local favorites and recent history. It has no ads, accounts, analytics service, or app backend.

For help, guide corrections, or privacy questions, email alhprocs@gmail.com.

A future public release

We plan a first public release with nonpersonalized Google AdMob ads. Ads and advertising-consent controls are not part of the current beta. The privacy policy and support instructions will be updated before that version is released.

Email links open your email app and do not send automatically. This website does not provide an app download.
