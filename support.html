A Little Handier Support

A Little Handier is published by Logan Wesney. For help, privacy questions, or a correction to a guide, email alhprocs@gmail.com.

These instructions describe the current iPhone TestFlight beta (build 2). It has no ads. Google AdMob is planned for a future public release but is not part of the current beta.

Reporting a problem

Please include what you can:

  • The guide title or screen, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem and any error message.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The app version and build shown under Privacy and local data.
  • An optional screenshot with personal information hidden.

Please do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information. You do not need to send your favorites or recent history. Support email is optional and uses your own email service. An email link opens your email app; it does not send automatically.

Using guides offline

The current beta's guides and photos are included with the app and can be used without an internet connection. Sending support email and submitting TestFlight feedback require a connection.

Clearing favorites and recent history

Open Privacy and local data, choose Clear favorites or Clear recent history, and confirm or cancel. Clearing one collection preserves the other. Use both controls to clear both.

If the app reports a storage error, the change may not have saved and older data may return when you reopen the app. Include the error message when contacting support. Backups or restores may retain or restore earlier copies; the app's clear controls do not erase backups.

These controls only update the app's local record. They do not delete support emails, TestFlight records, or website-host records.

TestFlight feedback

Beta testers can also submit feedback through TestFlight. Apple shares beta crash and usage information, and feedback you choose to send, with the developer. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

Future advertising-supported version

We plan to add nonpersonalized Google AdMob ads to the first public release. That version is not available through this website, and the current beta has no advertising-consent screen or Ad privacy choices control. We will update these instructions before releasing the ad-enabled version.

Support records and privacy

We delete our copies of support correspondence and downloaded beta reports within 90 days after the issue is closed, unless an unresolved dispute or legal requirement requires longer retention. Providers control their own records and backups. To request access or deletion, email alhprocs@gmail.com.

For details about local app data, support correspondence, TestFlight, this website, and planned advertising, read our Privacy Policy.

Safety

A Little Handier offers general guidance, not an emergency service or a substitute for a qualified professional. Stop if a task seems unsafe or is beyond your experience. For an urgent hazard, contact the appropriate emergency service or a qualified professional rather than waiting for an email reply.
