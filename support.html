A Little Handier Support

For help, privacy questions, or a correction to a guide, email alhprocs@gmail.com.

These instructions cover ad-free iPhone build 2 and Google AdMob test-ads build 3. Check your build under Privacy and local data. The build-3 advertising controls do not apply to build 2.

Reporting a problem

Please include what you can:

  • The guide title or screen, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem and any error message.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The app version and build shown under Privacy and local data.
  • An optional screenshot with personal information hidden.

Please do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information. You do not need to send your favorites or recent history. Support email is optional and uses your own email service. An email link opens your email app; it does not send automatically.

Using guides offline

Build 2's guides and photos are included with the app and can be used without an internet connection. Build 3 is designed to keep those guides available offline and when consent or ad loading fails. Ad requests, consent messages, support email, and TestFlight feedback require an internet connection.

Clearing favorites and recent history

Open Privacy and local data, choose Clear favorites or Clear recent history, and confirm or cancel. Clearing one collection preserves the other. Use both controls to clear both.

If the app reports a storage error, the change may not have saved and older data may return when you reopen the app. Include the error message when contacting support. Backups or restores may retain or restore earlier copies; the app's clear controls do not erase backups.

These controls only update the app's local record. They do not delete support emails, TestFlight records, or website-host records. In an ad-enabled build, they also do not change advertising consent or delete advertising-provider records.

TestFlight feedback

Beta testers can also submit feedback through TestFlight. Apple shares beta crash and usage information, and feedback you choose to send, with the developer. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

Build 3 test banner

Build 3 uses a Google-provided test banner on the Home screen only. Production ads remain disabled pending review. Build 2 has no banner. In build 3, the banner can be absent because of consent status, an offline connection, an ad-loading error, or a screen layout that cannot safely fit it. It is also hidden while searching or while Home is not the active screen.

When reporting an ad problem, include the build number, screen, approximate time, and whether the banner displayed the app's Test ad label. An optional screenshot with personal information hidden can help. Do not click an ad just to identify or report it.

Advertising privacy choices in build 3

In build 3, the consent check normally starts when Home first becomes eligible to show its banner during an app session, not necessarily immediately at launch. The app refreshes consent information and presents any required messages before requesting ads.

When required, open Privacy and local data → Ad privacy options to review or change your choices. If the check has not made ads ready, Retry ad privacy check appears when no check is in progress. It starts the check again. Neither control is available in build 2. On-device checks of build 3 remain pending as part of Owner Testing. The absence of a privacy-options button does not by itself mean no data is processed.

Build 3 requests nonpersonalized ads and does not request Apple's tracking permission. Google's advertising SDK can still process identifiers and data for SDK analytics. UMP processes consent-related data separately; see the privacy policy for both disclosures. Advertising choices are separate from clearing local guide records. The app is designed to keep guides usable without tracking approval or a successfully loaded ad.

We will update these instructions as beta testing identifies changes and before a public ad-supported release. This website does not provide an app download.

Support records and privacy

We delete our copies of support correspondence and downloaded beta reports within 90 days after the issue is closed, unless an unresolved dispute or legal requirement requires longer retention. Providers control their own records and backups. To request access or deletion, email alhprocs@gmail.com.

For details about local app data, support correspondence, TestFlight, this website, and planned advertising, read our Privacy Policy.

Safety

A Little Handier offers general guidance, not an emergency service or a substitute for a qualified professional. Stop if a task seems unsafe or is beyond your experience. For an urgent hazard, contact the appropriate emergency service or a qualified professional rather than waiting for an email reply.
