EarnPerJob privacy policy

Prepared November 16, 2026. Applies to the EarnPerJob subscription version.

Your business records

EarnPerJob stores the information you enter on your iPhone. This can include job names and dates, prices, costs, time, customer payment amounts, supplies, equipment, business bills and monthly income goals. Calculations run on your device. You do not create an EarnPerJob account, connect a bank account or send these records to an EarnPerJob server.

The app uses private iOS app storage and iOS file protection. It does not include advertising, tracking, third-party analytics or an AI service. The bundled record-management screens are blocked from making network requests. This does not prevent Apple's purchase services or links you choose to open from using the internet.

Backups and sharing

Your records may be included in normal iPhone backups according to your device settings. EarnPerJob does not provide its own cloud sync or remote recovery service.

When you choose Share backup, the app creates a readable JSON file and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose where to save it or whom to share it with. The selected app or service handles that copy under its own practices. The app attempts to remove its temporary export file when the share operation finishes; an interrupted operation may leave a temporary copy until it is removed by the system or app deletion. Exported backups are not password-protected by EarnPerJob. Share them only with a destination you trust.

You can also display backup text and copy it yourself. A copy placed on the clipboard or saved elsewhere is outside EarnPerJob's local record store. Restoring a backup replaces current records after confirmation; it does not merge them.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription purchases, payments and restoration through the App Store. EarnPerJob receives Apple-verified subscription information on your device, including the product, transaction identifier, validity dates and subscription status, to determine access. The app does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password, and it does not send your job records to Apple as part of a purchase.

EarnPerJob does not send subscription transactions to a developer-operated server or store subscription access in your business-record backup. Apple maintains its own purchase records and handles its services under Apple's App Store privacy notice.

Viewing and exporting existing records remain available when subscription access ends. Ending a subscription does not delete your records.

Retention and deletion

Records stay in the app's local storage until changed or removed. The app also retains a previous version of the saved document for recovery. Clear local records clears the active records, but may leave that previous copy and older-format local records. It is not a secure erase of all local copies. Removing the app using iOS Delete App, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data; copies in device backups, exported files, recipient services or the clipboard must be managed separately. Deleting the app does not cancel an App Store subscription.

There is no developer-held copy of the business records available for recovery through this app's current architecture.

Support and external pages

If you contact support, the information you voluntarily send is handled outside the local app. Avoid sending complete backups, customer information, payment details or other sensitive content unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.

Contact the app’s publisher at alhprocs@gmail.com for support or privacy questions, including requests about information you have sent us. Email links open your email app and do not send a message automatically.

If you email support, we receive your email address, any name you provide, your message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate the issue. This inbox uses Gmail, provided by Google; your own email provider also processes the message. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Support correspondence and any beta reports we download are retained for handling support requests, investigating issues, and addressing disputes or legal requirements. You can request deletion of information we hold by emailing the contact above. This policy does not set a fixed deletion deadline. Apple, Google and GitHub control their own service records and backups; removing our copies does not remove theirs.

TestFlight

For beta installations, Apple’s TestFlight service collects crash and usage information and shares it with the developer. Feedback and screenshots you choose to submit are also shared; your name/email may be included depending on how you were invited. This is separate from EarnPerJob’s local records. See Apple’s TestFlight privacy information. Apple controls its own service records.

These privacy/support pages use the existing GitHub Pages website. They add no analytics, advertising scripts, contact forms or tracking cookies. GitHub may log and store visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. External links use the destination’s privacy practices. Deleting information held by the publisher does not delete provider records, device backups or exported copies.
