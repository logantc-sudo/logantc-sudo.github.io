A few things to know

Who is EarnPerJob for?

Contractors, handymen, and other small service businesses that want a clearer picture of job costs and what's left from each job. Calculations use U.S. dollars.

Is this bookkeeping or tax software?

No. EarnPerJob provides planning estimates based on your entries. The amount left from a job is before your own pay and tax. It is not bookkeeping, tax filing, or professional accounting advice.

How does the subscription work?

An active Apple-managed subscription is required to create or edit records. Eligible new subscribers get one month free, then $4.99 per month in the U.S., renewing automatically unless canceled. Apple determines trial eligibility. Check the App Store purchase screen before confirming and manage or cancel your subscription through Apple. You can still view and export saved records without an active subscription.

Can I use it offline?

Saved records and calculations are local. Existing Apple-verified subscription access can continue while its verified validity remains current. Purchases, restoring purchases, and subscription updates may require an internet connection. See support for details.
