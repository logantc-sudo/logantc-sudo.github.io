EarnPerJob support

Prepared November 16, 2026. Applies to the EarnPerJob subscription version.

Need help?

Email alhprocs@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, EarnPerJob version and what happened. A screenshot without customer or financial details can help. Do not send your Apple Account password, payment-card details or a full business backup with an initial request.

Save and protect your records

Save changes before leaving a form. In Backup, use Share backup and choose a location you trust. Backups are readable JSON files, not password-protected files. Keep a separate copy before replacing your phone, deleting the app or restoring another backup.

Records are stored on your iPhone. Device backups may include them according to your iPhone settings, but EarnPerJob does not provide its own cloud sync or recovery service. Offloading and deleting an app are different; do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step.

Restore business records

Open Backup → Restore file, select an EarnPerJob JSON backup and review the confirmation. Restoring replaces current records; it does not merge them. Export current records first. Copy or restore text is another option for a complete backup.

If the app says saved records cannot be read, do not clear them immediately. Use Export unreadable saved text when offered, keep that copy and contact support with the error message. Do not send the exported contents unless needed and you choose to share them.

Restoring business records changes stored data and requires active subscription access in the subscription version. It is different from Restore purchases, which checks your App Store subscription and does not restore jobs.

Subscription help

Open Membership to see the current App Store price and whether your Apple account is eligible for a free trial. The configured US subscription is one month free for eligible new subscribers, then $4.99 per month, renewing automatically unless canceled. Apple determines trial eligibility. Check the purchase screen before confirming.

Already subscribed? Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. A connection to the App Store may be needed. If a purchase is pending approval, access starts after Apple verifies it.

Use Manage subscription to view or cancel renewal through Apple. Deleting EarnPerJob does not cancel the subscription. Viewing and exporting saved records remain available without active subscription access.

Working offline

Your saved records and calculations are local. Existing Apple-verified access can continue while its verified validity remains current. A new purchase, restoration, price lookup or subscription update may require internet access. If access cannot be confirmed, records remain available to view and export; reconnect and try Restore purchases or Retry connection.

Understanding the monthly plan

Enter your monthly business bills, the amount you want for yourself before personal tax, and typical job costs per $100 in sales. Sales to aim for the selected month estimates the sales needed to cover those amounts. It is a plan, not recorded profit. Use Avoid counting costs twice for examples. EarnPerJob provides USD planning estimates, not bookkeeping, tax filing or professional accounting advice.

Clear records carefully

Backup → Clear records → Clear local records clears active records after confirmation. In this version, a previous saved copy and older-format records may remain in local app storage. Exported files and device backups are not removed. This action does not cancel your subscription. Do not treat it as secure deletion of every copy.
